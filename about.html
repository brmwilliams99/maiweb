About

Masters AI Advisory is headed by Ben Masters Williams, a senior leader with more than 25 years of experience managing technology and security functions across professional services and enterprise environments in Europe, the UK, and Australia.

After early roles at Coca-Cola in Brussels and AMP in London, Ben moved into cybersecurity at McAfee, working as a trusted advisor to large enterprise clients including Barclays Bank and BAA Plc. He later relocated to Melbourne and spent 14 years at leading architecture firm Bates Smart, where he served as Head of IT and Associate Director.

During his tenure, he led a series of transformation initiatives, implemented a hybrid-cloud strategy, reduced IT costs for three consecutive years, managed the firm's technology response to the pandemic and built a technology function that consistently earned the trust of the business.

Masters AI Advisory has a clear understanding that new technology only matters if it is deployed responsibly and adopted by real people doing real work. We help organisations turn AI into practical outcomes that improve how they operate. Our focus is on real value, with a clear, vendor-neutral approach and governance built-in from the start.
