Governance

AI Governance & Risk

AI introduces operational risks that many organisations haven't encountered before and the regulatory landscape is shifting rapidly. We help you navigate this by aligning with internationally recognised standards, including the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO 42001, to meet evolving Australian expectations.

What we cover
  • AI policy and acceptable use frameworks
  • AI risk assessment and risk register development
  • Regulatory landscape briefings
  • Data governance and permissions review
  • Third-party and vendor AI risk
  • Shadow AI discovery
  • AI ethics and responsible use guidance
